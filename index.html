FAQ

Good to know

What age range do you teach?

Our programs are mainly designed for children, teens and young learners, with the exact program depending on age and current level.

How long does a track take?

There is no single fixed duration for every student. Progress depends on the learner's level, subject, pace and ability to apply what they learn independently.

1:1 or group — what's the difference?

1:1 is fully personalized to the student's pace. Group classes cover the same subject in a small-group environment at a lower fee when available.

Do we need any equipment?

Students need a laptop and an internet connection. Many programs use online tools and simulators.

Do you teach in Arabic?

Yes — classes are available in both Arabic and English.
